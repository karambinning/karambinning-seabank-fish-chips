About the shop

West Lynn isn’t King’s Lynn proper. It’s the quieter side — across the Great Ouse, on the way out to the salt marshes and The Wash. Seabank has been frying on St Edmundsbury Road long enough that a fair few of the regulars knew the shop before they knew their way home from it.

The recipe is the one a good chip shop always uses: a clean batter that crisps, not flakes; chips cut and cooked twice; fish that’s in the fryer the moment it’s ordered, not the moment it’s convenient. Nothing fancy. Nothing left under the lamp longer than it should be.

Walk in, ring ahead, or pop in on the way back from the post office next door. Open six days, closed on Sundays, and rated 5/5 by the Food Standards Agency for what that’s worth — which is, around here, quite a lot.
